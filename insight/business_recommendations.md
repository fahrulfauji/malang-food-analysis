# 💼 Business Recommendations

This document presents simple business-oriented recommendations derived from exploratory analysis of culinary market data in Malang City.

The purpose of these recommendations is to demonstrate how descriptive data analysis can support basic business interpretation and discussion.

---

## 📌 Recommendations for Restaurant Owners

### Menu Strategy

- Complementary menu items such as sambal appear frequently in customer discussions, suggesting that side dishes may contribute to customer dining satisfaction and menu uniqueness.

- Affordable menu pricing dominates the dataset, especially within the Rp25,000–Rp50,000 range, indicating that price accessibility plays an important role in the local culinary market.

- Restaurants offering balanced menu compositions across protein, carbohydrate, and complementary categories may provide broader customer appeal.

---

## 💰 Pricing Observations

- Most menu items fall within the mid-to-affordable price segment, reflecting the general pricing characteristics of restaurants included in the dataset.

- Premium-priced menu items appear less frequently, suggesting that the higher-end culinary segment represents a smaller portion of the sampled market.

- Higher pricing does not automatically correspond to higher restaurant ratings within this dataset.

---

## 🚀 Insights for New Culinary Entrepreneurs

- Seafood-themed restaurants show relatively strong customer engagement based on review volume.

- Developing signature side dishes or complementary menu items may help create differentiation in a competitive market.

- Entering the market with affordable pricing strategies may align more closely with the observed culinary patterns within the dataset.

---

## ⚠️ Analytical Scope & Limitations

These recommendations are based on descriptive and exploratory analysis only.

The analysis:
- focuses on observable patterns within the dataset,
- does not include predictive modeling or advanced statistical analysis,
- and does not consider operational costs, competition intensity, or seasonal demand factors.

The purpose of this document is to demonstrate how culinary data can be translated into simple business-oriented interpretation.
