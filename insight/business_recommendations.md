# 💼 Business Recommendations

This document presents business-oriented recommendations derived from exploratory analysis of culinary market data in Malang City.

The recommendations are intended to demonstrate how descriptive data analysis can support simple business interpretation and strategic discussion.

---

## 📌 Recommendations for Restaurant Owners

### Menu Strategy Insights

- Complementary menu items such as sambal appear frequently in customer discussions, suggesting that side dishes contribute significantly to overall dining satisfaction.
  
- Menus priced below Rp50,000 dominate the dataset, indicating strong consumer preference toward affordable and accessible dining options.

- Restaurants with balanced menu compositions across protein, carbohydrate, and complementary categories tend to maintain strong customer ratings and broader menu appeal.

---

### Pricing Observations

- Most menu items fall within the Rp25,000–Rp50,000 range, indicating that the mid-to-affordable segment dominates the culinary market in Malang.

- Premium-priced menus (above Rp75,000) appear less frequently in the dataset, suggesting that higher-end market positioning remains relatively limited.

---

## 🚀 Insights for New Culinary Entrepreneurs

- Seafood-themed restaurants show relatively high customer engagement based on review volume, indicating strong market interest within this category.

- Developing signature complementary items, such as sambal variations or side dishes, could serve as a differentiating factor in a competitive culinary market.

- Entering the market within the mid-price segment may align more closely with existing consumer behavior patterns observed in the dataset.

---

## 📊 Analytical Scope & Limitations

These recommendations are derived from descriptive and exploratory analysis techniques.

The analysis:
- focuses on observed patterns within the dataset,
- does not include predictive modeling or advanced statistical analysis,
- and does not account for external business factors such as operational costs, competition intensity, or seasonal demand fluctuations.

The purpose of this document is to demonstrate how raw culinary data can be translated into structured business-oriented insights.
